# What Works — Jacky Niche Playbook
*Last updated: 2026-06-04 (v4 — measured across all 4 dimensions + winning combinations)*

Built from **707 reels, 8 creators, ~90 days (Mar 6 – Jun 3 2026)**, with the full spoken transcript of
each. Every reel is classified on **four dimensions** — its **spoken hook** (first 5s), its **packaging
format** (how the info is delivered), its **topic** (what it's about), and its **emotional trigger** —
then each is ranked by **how consistently it wins** (win-rate + low flop-rate + sample size), not by its
single biggest hit. Winner/mid/flop = vs **each account's own median** (winner ≥3×, flop <0.5×).

Per-dimension detail + every cited reel:
[`hooks.md`](hooks.md) · [`formats.md`](formats.md) · `research/topic_trigger_ranking.json` ·
[`research/pattern-report-v4.md`](../research/pattern-report-v4.md). Reproduce all numbers with the
scripts in `research/` (0 API calls).

## The headline: winning is a COMBINATION

No single dimension wins alone — the reels that consistently beat their own median **stack a proven
hook + a proven format + a proven topic + a proven trigger.** The clearest winning stack in the data:

> **Topic = algorithm/reach mechanics · Format = settings-walkthrough · Trigger = hidden-lever-fix ·
> Hook = "Never do X until you fix these N…"** → **63–67% win-rate** across the reels that combine them.

The clearest losing stack: **Topic = hooks-and-scripting · Format = numbered-how-to list** → **0% win,
60% flop** (teaching "5 hooks" as a generic list is meta and saturated — see what-fails.md).

## 3. Two truths the cross-analysis exposes

**(a) The most COMMON content is the WEAKEST.** The three biggest categories by volume are the worst
performers — people pile into them precisely because they look easy:
- `mindset-and-business-philosophy` — **132 reels, 1,508 median views** (near-zero reach).
- `hooks-and-scripting` topic — **121 reels, 10% win / 38% flop** (everyone teaches hooks; it rarely wins).
- `relatability-validation` trigger — **112 reels, 10% win.**
**Do the opposite of the crowd:** tactical algorithm/settings/profile content is less crowded and wins more.

**(b) Reach ≠ reliability.** Some things travel far but inconsistently — bet on them knowingly:
- `ai-tools-and-commentary` (44.8K median views) and `category-taxonomy-explainer` (51K) get big reach but
  swingy win-rates. `gated-resource-tool` trigger has the 2nd-highest shares (188) but only 19% win.

## 4. Technical specs (from the winners)
| Spec | Recommendation | Basis |
|------|---------------|-------|
| Spoken hook (first 5s) | Outcome+time-box OR a specific rhetorical question | hooks.md: 69% / 29% win |
| Packaging | Show the taps (settings-walkthrough) or "if I started over" blueprint | formats.md: 53% / 50% win |
| Topic | Lean tactical: algorithm, profile/posting setup, reach levers | 58% / 50% win, low crowding |
| Trigger | Platform-FOMO, curiosity, or a hot-take — not generic relatability | 43% / 40% / 36% win |
| CTA | Comment-keyword in the **caption** only (never the spoken open) | caption mechanic |
| Share rate | Optimize >1.5% | cleanest cross-account signal |

## 5. The one-line rule
**Win = a specific outcome (hook) × shown-on-screen tactic (format) × a tactical reach topic × FOMO/
curiosity/hot-take (trigger).** Every confirmed winning stack is some version of that. The losers are
generic-topic + list-format + relatability — the easy, crowded default.
