## Currently Hot
*(first-noticed 2026-06-03 | last-confirmed 2026-06-03)*
- **Comment-keyword lead magnets** ("Comment GEAR/PROMPT/REC for…") — the dominant winning mechanic.
- **"Fix these N settings" + "Save this"** — tactical, save-driven Instagram-optimization posts.
- **AI-tool & AI-money angles** ("make $1M with AI", "Why I switched to Claude", "Guess the AI tools").
- **Instagram feature/update breakdowns** (trial reels, clickable links, new updates).
- **A/B "which video did better?"** curiosity posts.

---

## Rising
- **AI-money / AI-tool content** — @sam.gaudet's biggest hits ride this; expect more creators to follow.
- **New-feature-first content** — whoever explains an IG update *first* catches the wave (trial reels, clickable links).
- **"Steal/Save this template"** save-command framing (@alinamerkelcoach, @bhavinipanjwanii).

---

## Cooling
- **Generic clipping/podcast-news takes** ("Breaking: podcast sold for $200M") — @loganforsyth's flopped repeatedly; novelty wearing off unless tied to a viewer action.
- **Pure income flexes** — were a thing in this space; now consistently underperform.

---

## Dead
- **Generic motivational one-liners** ("ready is a decision", "stop consuming start creating").
- **Reposting a winner unchanged** — algorithm punishes it.

---

## Audience Is Asking For
*(inferred from what over-performs; confirm with comment data when budget allows)*
- **Done-for-you tools** — prompts, templates, equipment lists, song libraries. The resource IS the draw.
- **Exact account-level fixes** — settings, privacy toggles, posting mechanics.
- **First-mover intel on platform changes** — "what's new and what do I do about it."
