## 🟢 Proven-hot (high win-rate across many videos)
- **Outcome + time-box** ("[result] in 30 days / under 30s") — **56% win, N=16, 4 creators.** The most
  consistent format right now. e.g. @iamaayushswamy [DXPlUuDDNTk](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXPlUuDDNTk/), @realskytan [DYOLB7BCSC3](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYOLB7BCSC3/).
- **Save-prompted tactical tips** — **46% win, N=15.** "Save this before posting" + a fix.
- **Negative-command + number** — **46% win, N=13.** "Never post until you fix these 5 settings."
- **Rhetorical-question opens** — **N=123, all 8 creators.** The universal staple, net-positive.
- **Comment-keyword → DM** as the conversion layer — **N=180, 28% win.** Niche-wide standard.

## 🟡 Rising but unproven (watch — small samples)
- **Feature/IG-update breakdowns** — 66% win but **N=11 / 3 creators** and time-locked to releases. Spikes
  hard when it lands (1.65% median share). Post the day IG ships something; don't treat as a staple yet.
- **AI-tool / AI-money angles** — @sam.gaudet's lane (2.22M [DV3n5CvEjNQ](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DV3n5CvEjNQ/)); concentrated in one creator so far → rising, not yet broad.

## 🟠 Cooling / volatile
- **Shock-value / profanity openers** — **43% flop, N=48.** Was treated as hot off one big hit; the data says
  it's volatile. Cooling unless paired with real news + a brash brand.
- **Generic "clipping/podcast news"** takes (@loganforsyth) — repeated "Breaking:" framing flopped (0–3v) when
  not tied to a viewer action.

## 🔴 Dead (proven to fail)
- **Personal flexes** — 6% win / 68% flop. e.g. @alinamerkelcoach [DV1XgBTjU49](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DV1XgBTjU49/).
- **Generic motivation one-liners** — 5% win / 44% flop.
- **Reposting a winner unchanged** — repost fatigue (devinjatho's re-cuts: 322v vs the 521K original).

## Audience is asking for (inferred from what consistently over-performs)
- **Fast, specific, do-it-now fixes** — settings, prompts, templates, the exact next step.
- **Time-boxed outcomes** — "how fast can I get [result]" beats open-ended advice.
- **First-mover intel on platform changes** — explain a new IG feature before everyone else.

## Saturation signals
- **Comment-keyword CTA** is on 180/712 reels (25%) → saturating as a *mechanic*. Differentiation now comes
  from a **better spoken hook + a fresher resource**, not the CTA itself.
- **Negative-command hooks** are crowded (N=74) and flop 31% of the time when lazy — the **+number** version
  is the un-saturated edge.
