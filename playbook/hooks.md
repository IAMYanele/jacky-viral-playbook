## What counts as "the hook" here

**The hook = everything spoken in the first ~5 seconds** (the opening ~12–13 words of the Whisper
transcript). **Captions are excluded entirely** — every pattern below is detected on the **spoken audio
only**. The comment-keyword CTA ("comment GEAR…") is a **caption mechanic, NOT a hook**, so it is
removed from this ranking and reported separately at the bottom.

**Ranking = consistency:** win-rate + low flop-rate across a real sample (N) — not the single biggest
hit. Winner/mid/flop is judged vs **each account's own median** (winner ≥3×, flop <0.5×). Small-N
patterns with high win-rates are flagged **⚠️ promising, not proven**.

## The proven hooks (with full spoken evidence)

### S — Outcome + Time-Box  ·  N=13 · 4 creators · 69% win · 0% flop
The strongest spoken hook by a clear margin: **more than two-thirds beat 3× median and none flopped.**
A concrete result inside an explicit speed/effort frame, said in the first 5 seconds.

**Spoken templates (verbatim, first 5s):**
```
"This is the fastest way to [result] with no extra effort…"
"If I wanted to reach [goal] in the next 30 days, these are the only [N] things I'd…"
"How do you [outcome] in 60 minutes / 30 minutes? Step one…"
"Let's plan your next 30 days of content in 30 minutes…"
```
**Evidence — all 4 creators, big & tiny accounts:**
- 220,761v · winner · @iamaayushswamy [DXPlUuDDNTk](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXPlUuDDNTk/) — *"This is the fastest way to double your views with no extra effort."*
- 133,638v · mid · @personalbrandlaunch [DX9Sj-HO89h](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DX9Sj-HO89h/) — *"How do you create a month of content ideas in 60 minutes?"*
- 35,590v · winner · @realskytan [DYOLB7BCSC3](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYOLB7BCSC3/) — *"Let's plan your next 30 days of content in 30 minutes."*
- 13,751v · winner · @iamaayushswamy [DYf-dKgsCNm](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYf-dKgsCNm/) — *"If I wanted to reach 10K followers in the next 30 days, these…"*
- 31,186v · winner · @alinamerkelcoach [DY8LM_NtVoF](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DY8LM_NtVoF/) — *"10 hooks that always work. The fastest way to result is…"*
**This is the #1 hook to copy.**

### A — Rhetorical-Question Open  ·  N=106 · ALL 8 creators · 29% win · 18% flop
The only spoken hook used by **every creator**, on the largest sample, net-positive. Open by asking the
exact question the viewer wants answered. Specificity separates the 29% that win from the 18% that flop.

**Spoken templates:**
```
"How would you [achieve X] if you had to?"
"What's the [name] rule?" / "What happens if you…?"
"Is it still worth it to [decision]?"  /  "Did you know…?"  /  "Which grows faster, A or B?"
```
**Evidence (106 reels; spread across all 8):**
- 2,153,990v · winner · @devinjatho [DY5rxTQSxLy](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DY5rxTQSxLy/)
- 558,649v · winner · @personalbrandlaunch [DWE72LsIGQy](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWE72LsIGQy/) — *"Which video got more views? A or B?"*
- 168,507v · winner · @sam.gaudet [DYpS9k-Dz6B](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYpS9k-Dz6B/)
- 164,312v · winner · @bhavinipanjwanii [DWLhh_NKwgE](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWLhh_NKwgE/)
- 156,928v · winner · @sam.gaudet [DXsI2HIDLys](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXsI2HIDLys/) — *"What's the $50 to fix it rule?"*
- Note: @sam.gaudet's whole catalog opens this way. Generic questions ("want more views?") are where the 18% flop.

## Works, but execution-dependent (B-tier)

### Negative Command ("Never/Stop…")  ·  N=55 · 7 creators · 27% win · **36% flop**
High ceiling, **highest flop-rate of the broad hooks.** On its own it's a coin-flip-minus. The fix is the
number (see below).
- Win: 1,278,571v @devinjatho [DX0M0rOyEwI](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DX0M0rOyEwI/) — *"Never post on Instagram until you fix these five settings."*
- The 36% flop tail is bare "Never/Stop" with no number or payoff.

### Numbered List ("5 things…")  ·  N=40 · 7 creators · 17% win · 27% flop
Broad but **below-average as a spoken opener** — a number alone isn't a hook; it flops more than it wins.
Works only when the items are genuinely specific (and it's stronger fused with a negative command).
- Win: 1,278,571v @devinjatho [DX0M0rOyEwI](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DX0M0rOyEwI/). Mid/flop tail: generic lists from @personalbrandlaunch.

### Shock / Profanity Interrupt  ·  N=20 · 5 creators · 40% win · **40% flop**
**Wins and flops in equal measure.** It can spike hard (its hits double as the feature-news reels), but
it's volatile and brand-dependent — never a default.
- Spikes: 629,687v @devinjatho [DXo0c5bkn53](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXo0c5bkn53/), 521,470v [DVtNysZjc7I](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DVtNysZjc7I/).
- Flops: 322v @devinjatho [DVtNsdyjRJf](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DVtNsdyjRJf/) (same line, reposted).

## ⚠️ PROMISING — NOT PROVEN (high win-rate, small/narrow sample)

Eye-catching win-rates, but each fails the consistency bar — exactly the "one video popped" trap. Test
these; don't bank on them.

- **Feature/update news — 72% win, but N=11 / 3 creators, time-locked to IG releases**, and its top reels
  are the *same* devinjatho shock-interrupt reels (overlap). Post fast when IG ships something; not a staple.
  Evidence: [DXo0c5bkn53](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXo0c5bkn53/), [DWq2mazNwlP](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWq2mazNwlP/) (@alinamerkelcoach), [DWXjctoiQ-W](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWXjctoiQ-W/) (@iamaayushswamy).
- **Strip-it-back / reframe — 62% win, N=8.** Strong when it lands, but **half the wins are @devinjatho's
  one repeated "take off all my clothes" line** ([DXsJznEEsHp](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXsJznEEsHp/), [DYo0KHFSQrS](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYo0KHFSQrS/), [DY1v8ckS6Af](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DY1v8ckS6Af/)). Proven for that line; small as a general pattern.
- **Negative command + number — 55% win, N=9.** The upgrade to bare "Never/Stop" (27%→55%). Mechanism is
  clear; sample is small. [DX0M0rOyEwI](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DX0M0rOyEwI/), [DW5DTTQDHWX](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DW5DTTQDHWX/) (@iamaayushswamy).
- **Comparison A/B — 43% win, but only 2 creators** (@personalbrandlaunch + @iamaayushswamy). Reliable for
  them; not yet broad. [DWE72LsIGQy](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWE72LsIGQy/), [DXacxAyCalY](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXacxAyCalY/).
- **"You'll never go viral if…" — 83% win, N=6 — but it's basically ONE creator's ONE line, reposted.**
  4 of the 5 wins are @devinjatho reposting *"You will never go viral if you don't learn how to speak on
  camera"* ([DY5rxTQSxLy](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DY5rxTQSxLy/) + 3 re-uploads), +1 @iamaayushswamy on a different line, +1 @alinamerkelcoach **flop**. Proven for *that line*, not portable. Don't read 83% as transferable.

## Anti-pattern (spoken) + an honest caveat

- **Generic motivation** (spoken) — low win-rate, no tactic ("stop consuming, start creating"). Avoid.
- **Personal flex:** on *spoken* hooks this is only N=4 and mixed — because **the flex lives in the
  caption, not the spoken 5s.** Its strong failure signal (68% flop) came from caption analysis (see
  `what-fails.md`), not the spoken hook. Stated honestly so the evidence isn't overclaimed.

## Caption-layer mechanic (NOT a hook — reported separately)

**Comment-keyword CTA** — N=180 · 6 creators · ~29% win. This is a **caption/CTA layer** that harvests
comments → DMs; it rides on top of a spoken hook and is **not itself a stop-scroll.** Put it in the
caption + on-screen text. It cannot save a weak spoken open (e.g. @sam.gaudet "Comment 'hook'" on a flat
opener → 153v). It's deliberately excluded from the hook ranking above.

## What to actually say in the first 5 seconds (ranked by data)
1. **Outcome + time-box** (S, 69% win, 0% flop) — your highest-probability opener.
2. **Rhetorical question** (A, universal) — the everyday default; make it specific.
3. If using **"Never/Stop", attach a number** (lifts 27%→55% win).
4. **Test** feature-news (the week IG ships), reframes, and A/B — promising, not yet proven.
5. **Avoid** generic motivation; keep the **comment-keyword CTA in the caption**, never the spoken open.
