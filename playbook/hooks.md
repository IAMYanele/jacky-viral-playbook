## 🔑 The biggest finding: say one thing, caption another

Winners pair **a spoken pattern-interrupt hook** with **a separate keyword-CTA caption**. The video
*opens* with intrigue/value spoken aloud; the *caption* harvests the comment. Don't waste the spoken
open on "comment GEAR" — spend it on the hook, and put the CTA in the caption + on-screen text.

> **Evidence:** @devinjatho "Comment GEAR For My Equipment List" (caption)
> **Spoken hook:** *"If I take off all my clothes, if I remove this luxurious background behind me,
> swap my professional lighting for the room lights… I'll never be able to grab your attention."*
> **Metrics:** 4,686,071 views | 0.26% share — [DXsJznEEsHp](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXsJznEEsHp/)
> The caption tells you nothing about why it stopped the scroll. The *spoken* strip-it-back reveal does.

## Hook Pattern Rankings (Highest → Lowest) — by SPOKEN delivery

---

### 1. Rhetorical-Question / Self-Interview Open — **Tier S**

**Pattern:** Open by *asking the exact question the viewer wants answered*, then answer it fast —
often as a two-voice Q&A/interview cut. The question IS the hook.

**Spoken Templates:**
```
"If I wanted to [achieve big outcome], what should I do? First off…"
"What's the [name] rule? [Immediate answer]."
"Is it still worth it to [common decision]? [Contrarian answer]."
"What's the best [X] you ever made? [One-word answer], then proof."
```
*Proven spoken hooks:*
- *"If I wanted to go from zero to a million dollars using AI, what should I do? First off, cloud code."* — @sam.gaudet
- *"If you had to go from zero to a million followers in six months, how would you do it? Step one…"* — @personalbrandlaunch
- *"What's the $50 to fix it rule? Anybody can make a decision to solve a problem if it's less than $50."* — @sam.gaudet

**Why S:** @sam.gaudet's ENTIRE winning catalog uses this; @personalbrandlaunch's 427K "Ava" reel opens this way (caption was just "Comment Ava"). Q&A format gives instant payoff + implies authority.
**When it fails:** if the question is generic ("want more views?") rather than specific and high-stakes.
> **Evidence:** *"If I wanted to go from zero to a million dollars using AI…"* — @sam.gaudet
> **Metrics:** 2,215,348 views | 1.91% share — [DV3n5CvEjNQ](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DV3n5CvEjNQ/)

---

### 2. Negative Command + Number ("Never do X until you fix these N…") — **Tier S**

**Pattern:** A prohibition ("Never post until…") fused with a finite numbered fix. Loss-aversion +
specificity + save-ability. The single most *reused* winner structure (devinjatho ran it twice, both >1M).

**Spoken Templates:**
```
"Never post on Instagram until you fix these [N] settings. Number one…"
"Never, ever turn on these [N] settings…"
"Never [common action] without [the fix]."
```
*Proven spoken hooks:*
- *"Never post on Instagram until you fix these five settings. Number one, make sure your account isn't shadow banned."* — @devinjatho (1.28M, and a near-identical re-cut at 433K)
- *"Never, ever, ever turn on these three settings to increase your privacy…"* — @iamaayushswamy (21K vs 265 median)

**Why S:** appears as a top winner on 2 creators; pairs the strongest trigger (fear of an unseen mistake) with a save prompt.
> **Evidence:** *"Never post on Instagram until you fix these five settings…"* — @devinjatho
> **Metrics:** 1,278,571 views | 2.75% share — [DX0M0rOyEwI](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DX0M0rOyEwI/)

---

### 3. Shock / Profanity Pattern-Interrupt — **Tier A (highest share rates)**

**Pattern:** An outrageous or mildly profane spoken opener that breaks the polished-creator pattern.
Drove the **two highest share rates in the entire dataset (3.44%, 3.21%).**

*Proven spoken hooks:*
- *"Instagram just slapped their dick on the table with this new update. Because look, this video right here, you can actually click it."* — @devinjatho (521K, **3.44% share — #1 in dataset**)
- *"Instagram seriously just gave you the biggest f***ing cheat code and you didn't even notice…"* — @devinjatho (629K, 3.21% share)
- *"Instagram just dropped a massive new update. And while these creators will only tell you WHAT the update is, I'm gonna put y'all on how to use it to make a f*** ton of money."* — @iamaayushswamy (121K)

**Why A not S:** brand-dependent (fits a brash voice; off-brand for a polished coach). But where it fits, the share rates are unmatched.
**When it fails:** when it's shock with no payoff. Each example above immediately delivers a real tactic.
> **Evidence:** *"Instagram just slapped their dick on the table with this new update…"* — @devinjatho
> **Metrics:** 521,470 views | 3.44% share (highest in dataset) — [DVtNysZjc7I](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DVtNysZjc7I/)

### 4. Strip-It-Back / Counterintuitive Reveal — **Tier A**

**Pattern:** Open by *removing* the thing everyone assumes matters, to make a point about what
actually does. Cognitive tension.

*Proven spoken hooks:*
- *"If I take off all my clothes, remove this luxurious background, swap my $6,000 camera for my iPhone… I'll never be able to grab your attention. Because it's not what you say or how — it's who's saying it."* — @devinjatho (4.69M)
- *"What format goes the most viral? No matter the niche, no matter the account size… it is always the clone format."* — @personalbrandlaunch (231K)

> **Evidence:** 4.69M reel above — [DXsJznEEsHp](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXsJznEEsHp/)

---

### 5. Outcome Promise + Time Box — **Tier A**

**Pattern:** Concrete result + an explicit speed/effort frame.

*Proven spoken hooks:*
- *"This is the fastest way to double your views with no extra effort. Explained in under 30 seconds."* — @iamaayushswamy (221K)
- *"Let's plan your next 30 days of content in 30 minutes. It's called the 7-4 strategy."* — @realskytan (36K, named framework)

---

### 6. A/B Comparison Question — **Tier B–A**

**Pattern:** "Which did better, A or B?" Spoken aloud forces a guess (comment) + watch-to-reveal.
*Proven:* *"Which video do you think got more views? Video A or Video B?"* — @personalbrandlaunch (559K, 2.09% share). [DWE72LsIGQy](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWE72LsIGQy/)
*Caveat:* @realskytan ran "which video will do better?" and **flopped** (745v) — the difference is execution + audience size, so B-tier on its own.

## F-Tier — AVOID (now confirmed by SPOKEN audio)

**Personal flex / journey** (F): caption "I make six figures at 26."
> Spoken audio on @alinamerkelcoach's flops was literally **non-English reposts** ("6월 1일…", Korean) — wrong-language recycled content. 190–510 views. [DV1XgBTjU49](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DV1XgBTjU49/)
> **Lesson:** reposting foreign-language winners to an English audience dies; and personal-income framing flops regardless.

**Generic motivation** (F): caption "stop consuming, start creating."
> Spoken: *"Peanut butter jelly the long way…"* (no real hook). 194 views. [DYPdLGBSCOF](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYPdLGBSCOF/)

**"Comment X" with a weak spoken open** (F): @sam.gaudet "Comment 'Claude' for my prompt" → spoken open *"Editing videos. CapCut with AI captions."* (no hook) → 160v. **CTA can't save a flat spoken open.**

## ⚠️ Read-with-care: the "0-view" reels
Several flops at 0–3 views have **strong spoken hooks** (e.g. @iamaayushswamy *"Why is it so hard to write a good hook? Good thing I have these five that rarely fail"* → 0v). These read like **distribution failures (shadowban / brand-new account window), not bad content** — don't "learn" the wrong lesson from them. Weight flops with a real view base (hundreds–thousands) over true zeros.
