## Step 2 — Interview me (the most important part)

**Rules for how you interview:**
- Ask **one question at a time** and wait for my answer. Never dump a list of 10 questions.
- **Adapt** — dig into my answers, ask follow-ups, don't just run a script.
- Keep it **conversational and encouraging**, but be honest. I want the truth, not flattery.
- After every few answers, reflect back what you're hearing and **tie it to a specific playbook
  pattern** ("that lines up with the S-tier rhetorical-question hook" / "that's the personal-flex
  anti-pattern that flopped").
- If I give vague answers, push for specifics (real examples, real numbers, real hooks I've used).

**Cover these areas (in roughly this order, adapt as needed):**

1. **Who I am & my goal** — what's my business, who am I trying to reach, and what does a "win" look
   like (views? followers? leads? sales? booked calls?).
2. **My niche & angle** — what topics do I make content about, and what makes me different from the
   other people in my space.
3. **My current content** — what formats I post, how often, where my reels actually land
   (views/likes/shares). Ask me to describe my **last 3–5 reels and how they performed.**
4. **My hooks** — ask me to tell you the exact first line / first 2 seconds of a few recent reels.
   This is the highest-leverage area — compare what I say against the hook tiers in `hooks.md`.
5. **My CTA / funnel** — what I ask viewers to do, and whether I use the comment-keyword → DM
   mechanic (or anything to convert views into leads).
6. **What's working** — my best-performing reel ever, and ask me *why I think* it worked.
7. **What's not working** — where I feel stuck, what's flopped, what I've tried that didn't move.
8. **My production reality** — talking head? screen recordings? how much time/effort per reel? what
   tools? (so the plan is realistic for me).

## Step 3 — Diagnose me against the playbook

As we talk, build a picture of:
- Which **winning patterns** I'm already doing (reinforce these).
- Which **anti-patterns** I'm falling into (the things that reliably flop — e.g. personal flexes,
  generic motivation, CTA-in-the-spoken-open, reposting winners, vague hooks).
- The **gap between my hooks and the S/A-tier spoken hooks** in the playbook.

## Step 4 — Give me the deliverable

When you have enough (don't rush it — keep interviewing until you genuinely understand my situation),
produce a **Content Action Plan** with:

1. **Diagnosis** — 3–5 honest bullet points: what I'm doing right, and the biggest things holding me
   back, each tied to a playbook pattern with the evidence.
2. **My top 3 fixes** — the highest-leverage changes, in priority order, with *why* (cite the
   playbook) and *exactly how* to apply each to my content.
3. **5 hook rewrites** — take 5 of my real recent hooks (or topics) and rewrite them using the
   S/A-tier spoken-hook templates from `hooks.md` (rhetorical-question, negative-command + number,
   strip-it-back reveal, etc.). Show before → after.
4. **A 1-week content plan** — 3–5 specific reel ideas I could film this week, each with: the spoken
   hook to open with, the format, and the comment-keyword CTA for the caption.
5. **What to stop doing** — the anti-patterns I should cut immediately.

Keep the whole plan specific to MY business and MY answers — no generic filler. If anything about my
situation is still unclear before you write the plan, ask me one more round of questions first.
